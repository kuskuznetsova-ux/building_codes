# 2.16 High-rise buildings: thresholds and key requirements

!!! warning "Status: draft"
In Russia a building is high-rise if it is **above 75 m**. The table compares the threshold in other countries; below is a matrix of key requirements. Each cell leads to a topic page: the original on the **left** (black), the Russian translation on the **right** (grey). **⏳** — not verified.

## How to read

- Thresholds are **not directly comparable**: Russia — height per SP 1.13130; Serbia — top-floor level above the fire-service platform; England — to the top of the top-storey floor plus counting of storeys; Germany — mean level over terrain to the floor of the top storey with a habitable room.
- England has “high-rise” notions at two levels: a **higher-risk building** under the Building Safety Act (18 m / 7 storeys) is about the procedure (gateways), while the technical fire-safety thresholds are in Approved Document B (11, 18, 30 m).
- For the EU the portal includes the German example (MBO and the Muster-Hochhaus-Richtlinie, 2008 edition — model acts, the Länder make changes). France etc. — in the list below.
