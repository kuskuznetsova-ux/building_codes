## How to read

- Documents **cannot be compared “number to number”**: countries have different building classes, methods of measuring distances and different legal force. That is why the matrix starts with three “bases of comparison”: [building classes](klassy.md), [methods of measuring height and distances](metodiki.md) and [legal force of documents and permitted deviations](pravovaya-sila.md) (SP, pravilnik, Approved Document, possibility of STU).
- The **EU** has no uniform fire-safety norms for buildings: Regulation (EU) No 305/2011 (CPR) sets only general requirements for construction works (Annex I, cl. 2). The EU column therefore shows the CPR and, where available, an example of a national norm from **Germany** (Musterbauordnung, MBO). The MBO is a model code; each federal state has its own law.
- **UAE (Dubai)**: the Dubai Building Code 2021 (DDA copy) gives architectural and some fire requirements, while most fire norms are referred to the UAE Fire and Life Safety Code (Civil Defence), which is unavailable ⏳ — so for many rows the UAE cell says “not verified”.
- Russia: SP 1.13130.2020 (evacuation), SP 4.13130.2013 (limiting fire spread), FZ-123 (general requirements). Serbia: Pravilnik on technical norms for fire protection of residential, business and public buildings (“Sl. glasnik RS”, 22/2019). England: Approved Document B, Volume 1 (dwellings).
