## How to read

- First pass — general requirements of sections 4–5 of SP 118 (scope, room height, stairs, lifts). The special chapters by building type (education, health care, retail, entertainment buildings) are not included.
- Many countries have no uniform national norms on room height and steps — there ⏳ or state/municipal norms are shown.
- Fire requirements for public buildings — in section [2. Fire safety](../pozharnaya/index.md); accessibility — in section [7.1](../dostupnaya/index.md); parking — in section [7.3](../parkovki/index.md).
