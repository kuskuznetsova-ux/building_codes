# 7. Architecture

!!! warning "Status: draft"
This section collects requirements for the architectural design of buildings. It currently has six subsections; the rest (layout solutions, functional groups, general requirements) is a placeholder.

## Subsections

- [7.1 Accessible environment](../dostupnaya/index.md) — SP 59.13330.2020 and analogues: ramps, doors, lifts, parking, toilets, housing.
- [7.2 Public buildings](../obshchestvennye/index.md) — SP 118.13330.2022: room height, stairs, lifts, entrances, workplaces, halls, teaching rooms, driveways, atriums.
- [7.3 Parking](../parkovki/index.md) — SP 113.13330.2023: types of car parks, bay sizes, ramps, built-in car parks, evacuation, fire resistance, charging stations.
- [7.4 Residential high-rise buildings above 75 m](../vysotnye-zhilye/index.md) — SP 267 (section 6), SP 477 → SP 550: room height, vestibules, guards, windows, roof, service rooms.
- [7.5 Public high-rise buildings above 50 m](../vysotnye-obshchestvennye/index.md) — the same for public and multifunctional buildings.
- [7.6 Residential buildings up to 75 m](../zhile-do-75/index.md) — SP 54.13330.2022: scope, flat areas, stairs, windows, accessibility.

Public buildings up to 75 m (below the high-rise threshold) — see [7.2](../obshchestvennye/index.md) (SP 118).

The matrices compare Russia, Serbia, the EU (Germany, France, Austria, Spain), England, the UAE (Dubai) and Georgia. Fire requirements for these buildings are in section [2. Fire safety](../pozharnaya/index.md).
