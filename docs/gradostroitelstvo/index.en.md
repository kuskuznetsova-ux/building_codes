## How to read

- **Insolation** (direct sunlight) is regulated in Russia by hours and latitude zones; in Serbia, the EU and England the laws have no such strict hourly norms — standards (EN 17037) and guides (BRE BR 209) or general rules on mutual overshadowing are used.
- **Distances between buildings** under the master plan (domestic gaps, insolation-based calculation) are a separate topic from **fire separation distances** (see the [fire matrix](../pozharnaya/razryvy.md)).
- SP 42.13330.2026 replaced SP 42.13330.2016; some requirements in the text were moved to references to SanPiN and Federal Law 384.
